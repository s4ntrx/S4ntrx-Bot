# Phishing

## Definition
Phishing is a social-engineering attack where an attacker impersonates a
trusted party (a bank, a company, a coworker) — usually by email, SMS, or
a chat message — to trick you into revealing credentials, clicking a
malicious link, or sending money.

## Common indicators
- Urgency or threats ("your account will be closed in 24 hours")
- Requests for passwords, OTPs, or financial details
- Links that don't match the sender's real domain
- Generic greetings ("Dear Customer") instead of your name
- Unexpected attachments
- Spelling and grammar mistakes
- Requests to keep the matter secret from IT or a manager

## What to do if you suspect phishing
1. Don't click links or open attachments.
2. Don't reply or provide any information.
3. Verify through a separate, known-good channel (call the company directly).
4. Report it to your school's IT/security administrator.
5. If you already clicked a link or entered credentials, change the
   password immediately from a different, trusted device and enable MFA.

## Spear phishing
A targeted version of phishing aimed at a specific person, often using
information gathered about them (job title, coworkers, recent projects)
to appear more convincing.
