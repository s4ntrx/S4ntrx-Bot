# Ransomware

## Definition
Ransomware is malware that encrypts a victim's files and demands payment
(usually in cryptocurrency) for the decryption key.

## How it typically spreads
- Phishing email attachments
- Malicious downloads or cracked software
- Unpatched software vulnerabilities
- Compromised remote-access credentials

## If you suspect ransomware
1. Disconnect the device from the network immediately.
2. Do not restart the device — some ransomware causes more damage on reboot.
3. Do not pay the ransom.
4. Photograph the ransom note/screen for evidence.
5. Report to your school's IT/security administrator right away.
6. Recovery normally requires restoring from backups.

## Prevention
- Keep regular, offline/disconnected backups
- Keep operating systems and software patched
- Don't open attachments from unknown or unexpected senders
- Use MFA on all accounts with remote access
